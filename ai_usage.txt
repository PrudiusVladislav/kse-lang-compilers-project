  MINE — checker and codegen

  Signature, location, IR shape only.

  Node fields you get to work with

  ProgramNode — .structs, .functions, .statements, .exit. The first two are
  empty lists for a pre-practice-6 program.

  StructNode — .name, .fields (ordered FieldNodes, index = position), .line/.col
  at the name.
  FieldNode — .name, .type_name ("i32"/"i64"/"bool" or a struct name,
  unresolved), .mutable, .line/.col at the field name. That position is what
  M3's "first non-mut link" wants.

  FnNode — .name, .params, .result_type, .body (a BlockNode whose .exit is
  guaranteed non-None), .line/.col at the name.
  ParamNode — .name, .type_name, .mutable hardcoded False, so seeding params
  into a scope frame makes := on a param hit the existing "is not mut" error for
  free.

  DeclNode — .values is now a list (one element for a built-in, one per field
  for a struct), .brace is the { token for the arity error, plus .name,
  .type_name, .mutable. .type_name may be a struct name; nothing resolves it
  yet. .init is a property returning values[0] — a shim so the untouched checker
  and codegen kept running; delete it when M2 learns about the list.

  AssignNode — .name, .path (list of ident tokens, empty for a plain assign),
  .value. .line/.col at the root ident; each token in .path carries its own
  position.

  FieldAccessNode — .name, .path (same token list, never empty), .line/.col at
  the root.
  CallNode — .name, .args, .line/.col at the name.
  InitNode — .values (may contain nested InitNodes), .line/.col at its own {,
  which is where the handout wants both bonus errors reported.

  Two notes for your side. The visitor methods visit_struct, visit_field,
  visit_fn, visit_param, visit_field_access, visit_call, visit_init are
  referenced by accept but don't exist on either pass yet — that's M1–M11. And
  parse_type accepts any ident as a type name, so Foo p{1} parses fine and
  unknown type 'Foo' is the checker's error to raise, which is also what makes
  self-reference and forward-reference fall out without their own check.
